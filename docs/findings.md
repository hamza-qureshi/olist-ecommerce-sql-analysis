# Findings

## Q1 — Monthly revenue trend
Question: How is monthly revenue trending, and what is the month-over-month growth?  
Finding: Revenue increased from R$138,488 in Jan 2017 to a peak of R$1,194,883 in Nov 2017, before easing to R$878,401 in Dec 2017. The pattern shows strong growth through the year with a major spike in the year-end shopping season.

## Q2 — Top 10 product categories
Question: Which product categories drive the most orders and revenue?  
Finding: The top 10 product categories generated R$9,619,616 in revenue. Together, they accounted for 63.2% of total delivered revenue, showing that sales are concentrated in a relatively small part of the catalog.

## Q3 — RFM customer segmentation
Question: Which customer segments are most valuable and which are at risk?  
Finding: 32.38% of customers were classified as “At-Risk” under the RFM framework. This segment represented R$5,013,170.04 in recoverable revenue, making it the clearest retention target.

## Q4 — Cohort retention
Question: How well do customer cohorts retain over time?  
Finding: The cohort table shows 100% retention at month 0 for each cohort, but retention falls sharply in later months. This means most customers do not return for repeat purchases, and repeat activity declines quickly after the first order.

## Q5 — Delivery time vs review score
Question: How does delivery speed affect customer review scores?  
Finding: Orders delivered within 7 days averaged 4.41 stars, while orders taking more than 30 days averaged 3.17 stars. Faster delivery is clearly associated with better customer satisfaction.

## Q6 — Seller performance ranking
Question: Which sellers generate the most revenue overall and within each state?  
Finding: The top seller generated R$165,981.49 from 319 orders and ranked first overall as well as first in SP. The highest-revenue sellers are heavily concentrated in São Paulo.

## Q7 — Geographic revenue distribution
Question: Which customer states generate the most revenue and orders?  
Finding: São Paulo (SP) led with R$5,769,703.15 across 40,501 orders, followed by Rio de Janeiro (RJ) at R$2,055,401.57 and Minas Gerais (MG) at R$1,818,891.67. Revenue is strongly concentrated in a small number of states.

## Q8 — Order funnel
Question: What share of orders reach delivered status versus being canceled?  
Finding: Out of 99,441 total orders, 96,478 were delivered, producing a 97.02% delivery rate, while 625 were canceled, equal to a 0.63% cancellation rate. The fulfilment process is highly successful overall.
